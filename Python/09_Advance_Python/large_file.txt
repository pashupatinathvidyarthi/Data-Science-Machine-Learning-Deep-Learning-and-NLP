Smt. Droupadi Murmu is the 15th and current President of India, serving since 25 July 2022.
Smt. Droupadi Murmu, born on 20 June 1958 in Uparbeda village, Mayurbhanj district, Odisha, belongs to the Santali tribal community and is the first tribal woman to hold the office of President of India. She is also the second woman to serve as President, after Pratibha Patil, and the youngest to assume the role at age 64. Murmu previously served as the Governor of Jharkhand from 2015 to 2021, becoming the first tribal woman governor of a state with a significant tribal population. 

Early Life and Education
Murmu was born into a Santhal tribal family and faced significant personal hardships, including the loss of her husband and two sons. She completed her primary education in her village and later moved to Bhubaneswar for higher studies. She graduated with a Bachelor of Arts degree in Political Science and Economics from Rama Devi Women’s University in 1979.

Political Career
Murmu began her political journey as a councillor of Rairangpur Nagar Panchayat in 1997 and later became a member of the Odisha Legislative Assembly from 2000 to 2009. She served as Minister of State (Independent Charge) for Commerce and Transport (2000–2002) and Fisheries and Animal Resources Development (2002–2004) in the Odisha government. She joined the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) and held leadership roles in the party’s tribal wing before being appointed Governor of Jharkhand.

Presidency
Murmu was elected President of India in July 2022 with the support of the National Democratic Alliance (NDA), defeating opposition candidate Yashwant Sinha with 676,803 electoral votes, representing 64.03% of the total. As President, she serves as the head of state, the first citizen of India, and the supreme commander of the Indian Armed Forces. While the role is largely ceremonial, the President has constitutional powers to uphold the Constitution, give assent to bills, summon and dissolve Parliament, and promulgate ordinances when necessary. 


Key Achievements and Initiatives
During her tenure, Murmu has focused on promoting education, especially for girls from marginalized communities, preserving India’s cultural and linguistic diversity, and engaging with the Indian diaspora through state visits abroad. She has been honored with several international awards and honorary doctorates for her contributions to public service and leadership.

Historical Context
The office of the President of India was established on 26 January 1950, replacing the Governor-General as the constitutional head of state. Rajendra Prasad was the first President, serving two terms from 1950 to 1962. Since then, India has had 15 Presidents, with Murmu being the current incumbent. 


Droupadi Murmu’s presidency is notable for breaking multiple barriers, representing tribal communities, and emphasizing inclusive governance while upholding the constitutional framework of India.
